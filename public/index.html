About Blue Lake Construction

Blue Lake Construction is a residential remodeling and general contracting company serving homeowners throughout Salt Lake City and the surrounding Salt Lake Valley. Associated with Ryan Gardner a licensed contractor (license number 6085795-5501) and Amy Gardner as the administrative contact for Blue Lake Enterprises Inc.

Published resources cover Full Home Remodels, Whole-House Renovations, Interior Remodeling, Floor Plan Reconfiguration, Open-Concept Conversions, Kitchen Remodels, Bathroom Remodels, Basement Remodeling, Flooring Replacement, Interior Painting, and 49 additional service areas.

Service coverage includes Salt Lake City, Sugarhouse, Millcreek, Cottonwood heights, Sandy, Draper, Riverton, West Jordan, South Jordan, Taylorsville and 2 more communities.
